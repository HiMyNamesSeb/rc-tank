# Phase 3: Wireless control (ESP32 Bluetooth)

**Status:** ⏳ Not started

## What
Move the brains from the Nano to an ESP32 and control the drive, turret and barrel over Bluetooth from my phone.

## Why
Bluetooth is cheaper and easier than a hobby RC transmitter, and range isn't a concern for this tank.

## Goal
Everything controllable from the phone with no wires to the computer.
